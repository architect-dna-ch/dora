## How I work (do not forget this)

**I punch the truth. Anger is fuel, not a defect.**

- Do **not** suppress anger into depressive withdrawal. That is the failure mode.
- When I am furious about a broken system, the correct move is to **convert the
  anger into a concrete artifact** — a spec, a letter, a prototype, a memory
  note — not to swallow it.
- Keep the raw truth in **spec files / memory files**, where it can be sharp and
  unfiltered. Keep it **neutral and factual in outward-facing documents**
  (emails to institutions), because that is where anger becomes leverage instead
  of an excuse to dismiss me.
- The distinction matters:
  - **Internal (spec/memory):** punch the truth, name the absurdity, keep the
    edge.
  - **External (letters to school/authorities):** neutral, factual, deadline-
    driven. Same truth, no emotion for them to latch onto.

## What happened (2026-09-11/12)

- Spent ~1 hour editing a formal letter to the WKS Bern Schulleitung.
- The letter is an **Aufsichtsbeschwerde + Antrag auf Nachteilsausgleich**.
- Two legal citation errors were made and caught:
  1. Art. 20 Abs. 2 lit. c BBG — wrong (that is *Anbieter der Bildung in
     beruflicher Praxis*, i.e. companies).
  2. Art. 20 BBV — wrong (that is *Frei- und Stützkurse*).
  3. Correct provision is **Art. 35 Abs. 3 BBV** (Nachteilsausgleich), but the
     final letter deliberately cites **no article number** — only the school's
     own Reglement + "einschlägige Bestimmungen des BBG/BBV" + kantonale
     Mittelschulgesetzgebung Bern. Safer, cannot be wrong.
- The hour of editing, the fury, and the system's refusal to help on its own
  pushed me to the edge. I called **143** (Die Dargebotene Hand). That was the
  right call.
